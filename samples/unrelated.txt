The Joy of Cooking Rice

Rice is the staple food of millions of homes across Asia. To cook a perfect pot, begin by washing the grains in cold water until it runs almost clear. This removes the extra starch that would otherwise make the rice sticky and heavy.

Next, measure the water carefully. Most varieties of long grain rice need about one and a half cups of water for every cup of rice. Bring the water to a boil, add a pinch of salt, lower the flame, and cover the pot with a tight lid. Do not lift the lid while it cooks, because the steam is what finishes the job.

After fifteen minutes, switch off the heat and let the pot rest for five more minutes. Fluff the grains with a fork and serve them warm with lentils, vegetables or a simple curry.
